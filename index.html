LyricFlow Studio - User Guide & Reference

Comprehensive manual for synchronized lyrics video production

1 The 5-Step Creation Process

LyricFlow Studio creates high-definition synchronized lyric videos in 5 streamlined steps:

  1. Step 1 (Media Pool & Audio): Load your song (MP3/WAV) and upload background videos, images, or choose live audio-reactive visuals.
  2. Step 2 (Lyrics & Delimitation): Paste or load lyrics, choose grouping mode, format text, and save changes back to disk.
  3. Step 3 (Style & Layout): Select 16:9, 9:16, or 1:1 format, customize fonts, stroke outlines, box highlights, and set 6-point custom tap positions.
  4. Step 4 (Live Studio Recording): Hit Record & Spacebar to tap lyrics in real time while singing or listening.
  5. Step 5 (Export & Playback): Instant hardware-accelerated HD video generation ready for download!

Audio Tracks & Background Pool

  • • Audio Formats: Supports standard MP3, WAV, AAC, and FLAC files with real-time waveform & spectrum telemetry.
  • • Video Backgrounds: MP4 and WebM motion loops. Video speed can be slowed down from 0.125x (1/8 speed) to 3.0x in Live Studio.
  • • Image Slideshows: Upload multiple images at once. The app sorts them naturally and enables auto-slide advancing with Shift+Space or cue tags.
  • • Audio Reactive Visuals: Choose from 5 live procedural styles (Cyber Liquid Aurora, Fluid Waveforms, Silk Streams, Ambient Nebula, Tidal Swells) with dynamic color palettes that pulse to your bass & rhythm.

Lyrics Formatting & Media Tags

  • • Automatic [Verse n] Stripping: Lines like [Verse 1], [Verse 2], [Chorus], or [verse n] are automatically filtered so section labels don't clutter your cue stream.
  • • Punctuation Cleaning: On-screen display strips distracting commas, periods, colons, and semi-colons while keeping exclamation marks, questions, and contractions intact.
  • • Auto Slide Switch Tag: Add [slide: filename.jpg] or [slide: 2] or [slide] to any cue to trigger a specific background slide automatically when that lyric is fired!
  • • Auto Video Switch Tag: Add [video: filename.mp4] or [video] to switch motion backgrounds automatically with lyrics.
  • • Save Lyrics Button: Directly write your edited text and formatting back to your file on disk.

Styling, Positioning & Tap Points

  • • Aspect Ratios: 16:9 Landscape for YouTube, 9:16 Vertical for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, 1:1 Square for Instagram.
  • • Typography & Legibility: Outlines, drop shadows, and pill box backgrounds ensure crisp readability across bright or chaotic background footage.
  • • 6-Point Custom Tap Positioning: Switch positioning to "Tap Points (1-6)", then click anywhere on the live preview screen to place ordered landing markers. When recording, your lyrics will glide dynamically through your custom points!
  • • Transitions: Choose between Fade, Smooth Crossfade, Slide Up, Zoom Punch, or Instant Appear.

Live Recording Studio Controls

  • • Spacebar: Advances to the next lyric cue instantly.
  • • Shift + Spacebar: Advances lyric cue AND switches to the next slideshow photo simultaneously.
  • • B Key: Toggles blank cue (temporarily clears text from screen for instrumental solos).
  • • 1 to 9 Keys: Switch background visual assets live on the fly during recording.
  • • Left Arrow [←] / Right Arrow [→]: Step backward and forward through cues.
  • • Auto-Stop Limit: Choose to stop recording automatically when audio ends, after 30s/60s, or specify custom seconds.
